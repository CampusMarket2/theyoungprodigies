# The Young Prodigies

Minimalist luxury fashion storefront rooted in Kumasi, Ghana.

## Features
- **Floating Welcome Curtain**: Atmospheric first-visit entrance with drifting light orbs and smooth curtain reveal.
- **Video-First Homepage**: Autoplaying editorial fashion films replacing static homepage banners.
- **Lookbook, Shop & About Us**: Editorial lookbook with direct "Shop This Look" modals, filterable store catalog, and multi-chapter brand story.
- **Ghana Cedi (GH₵) & Zone Delivery**:
  - Within Kumasi: **GH₵ 20.00**
  - Outside Kumasi (Nationwide): **GH₵ 40.00**
- **Paystack Checkout**: Supports **Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money)** and **Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)**.

## Setup Paystack Live Payments
1. Open `js/data.js`.
2. Replace `'pk_test_YOUR_PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY'` with your Paystack Public Key (`pk_live_...` or `pk_test_...`).
3. Commit and push your changes.
